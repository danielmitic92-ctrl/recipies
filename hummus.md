# Hummus
**Hummus is great!**
## Ingredients
- Soy
- Noodles
- Chili
- Salt
- Pepper
- Tahin
- Garlic
- Olive oil

## Instructions
- Puree chickpeas
- Add everything else into blender
- Blend until desired consistency
- Tip: Add ice cubes to make it creamier
