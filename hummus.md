# Hummus
**Hummus is great!**
## Ingredients
- Chickpeas
- Vinegar
- Lemon
- Tahin
- Garlic
- Olive oil

## Instructions
- Puree chickpeas
- Add everything else into blender
- Blend until desired consistency

## Serving tips
Hummus is great to eat with bread, but also as a game night snack with cucumber and carrot sticks.
